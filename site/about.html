About Habesha Exam Center

Habesha Exam Center is a free study website for Ethiopian students preparing for school exams. It offers past-style practice exams, study resources such as textbooks, teacher guides and videos, and a place to track your results.

What the site offers

  • Practice exams by grade and subject, with answers and explanations.
  • Learning resources: textbooks, teacher guides and video lessons.
  • An account to save your results. Accounts are optional for browsing.

Who runs the site

The site is run by the Habesha Exam Center student team. It is free to use and does not charge students or sell software.
