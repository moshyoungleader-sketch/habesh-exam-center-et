STUDENT CONTRIBUTION PROGRAM

Help students learn.
Share useful resources.

Apply to join the Habesha Exam Center student team collecting, organizing and sharing exam papers and study notes for students in Grades 6, 8 and 12.

📚📝🎓

Who can apply?

🌱

Grade 6

Help gather primary-level exam papers, short notes and revision materials.

📘

Grade 8

Contribute middle-school exam papers, subject notes and useful study resources.

🎓

Grade 12

Help organize preparatory-level exam papers and subject revision notes.

What will contributors do?

  • Collect relevant exam papers and study notes.
  • Check that files are readable, clearly named and organized by grade and subject.
  • Share only materials that may be legally and ethically distributed.
  • Help make useful resources easier for students to access, including for offline study.
