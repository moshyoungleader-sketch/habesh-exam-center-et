Privacy

This page explains what information the Habesha Exam Center website collects, why, and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored in protected form, not as plain text.
  • Profile choices: grade, region and stream, if you choose to give them.
  • Exam results: your answers and scores, so you can review your progress.

How we use it

We use this information only to run your account, save your results and show your progress. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Account and results are stored in a secure database hosted by Neon (Postgres) and connected through Vercel. Some information is also kept in your browser so the site works offline.

Your choices

You can view and update your profile in Settings, and log out at any time.
